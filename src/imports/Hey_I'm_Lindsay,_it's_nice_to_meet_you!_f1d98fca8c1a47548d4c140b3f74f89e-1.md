## My Approach to Product Design

✅ **Research First, Research Often**	

Utilize research for identifying knew opportunities and keep learning throughout a project's lifecycle using appropriate research methodologies based on project constraints, timelines, and learning objectives.

🤏 **Incremental Changes**

Design towards a future without waiting for it. Provide users value with small improvements over time.

🤲 **Cultivate Collaboration**

Every team member’s voice is valuable. Work hard to build relationships, encourage engagement from others, and bring them along for the ride.

👂**Consider Your Audience**

Curate communications based on who’s listening. Find the right story to tell in an engaging way that is unique to your audience.

🥧 **Be Holistic**

Individual user tasks are often inter-connected, it's best to consider the total impact across product feature teams and all platform areas when making design decisions.

🥤**Evolve and Be Slushee**

Reflect, evaluate, and always be changing. Experiment with new processes to improve and optimize team collaboration and effectiveness.
